## SEO in Oliver: common questions

Straight answers to the questions Edmonton business owners ask us most.

### Can we offer discounts for reviews?

No. Google's contribution policy does not allow rewards such as cash, discounts or free services in exchange for reviews. It also does not allow asking only happy customers. We help you ask every customer, honestly.

### What if we get a bad review?

Reply calmly, acknowledge the issue and offer to resolve it offline. Future customers read your replies.

### How many reviews do we need?

There is no fixed number. Steady, recent reviews matter more than a one-time spike.
