## What Google says about titles and snippets

Google's SEO Starter Guide recommends that every page has a unique, clear, concise and accurate title, and that meta descriptions, when used, are short, unique and relevant.

It also confirms keyword stuffing goes against Google's spam policies. We write for people first and let relevance follow.

## What matters and what does not

| Common belief | What Google says |

|---|---|

| You need the keywords meta tag | Google Search does not use it |

| Longer pages rank better | There is no word count target |

| Keywords in the domain help a lot | They have hardly any ranking effect |

| Repeat the keyword many times | Keyword stuffing violates spam policies |

| Headings must be in perfect order | Out of order headings do not matter for Search |
