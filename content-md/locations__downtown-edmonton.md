## Storefront or service area: set it up right

Businesses that welcome customers at an address and businesses that travel to customers need different Business Profile setups.

Getting this right is the first step for any Downtown business, because it decides whether Google shows your address, your service area or both.

- Storefront: address and hours shown

- Service area: areas you serve listed

- Hybrid: both, set up carefully
